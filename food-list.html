Ingredients for Amala and Ewedu

  • Yam flour
  • Ewedu leaves
  • Water
  • Locust beans
  • Salt
  • Palm oil
  • Pepper
  • Seasoning

Steps in Cooking Amala and Ewedu

Step 1
Boil water in a pot for the Amala
Step 2
Pour the flour into the boiling water and stir continuosly
Step 3
Continue stirring until the Amala becomes smooth and properly cooked
Step 4
Wash the Ewedu leaves and cook them in a pot
Step 5
Add locust beans, pepper, seasoning and salt to the Ewedu
Step 6
Blend the Ewedu until it becomes smooth
Step 7
Serve the Amala with the Ewedu soup.
